### Marine Genomics

This repository contains my workshop activities and bioinformatics analyses for the **Marine Genomics** module. It documents my progression from basic sequence analysis to more advanced metagenomic workflows, with an emphasis on reproducibility, genome assembly, taxonomic classification, annotation, and interpretation of marine microbial communities.

### Repository Content

The workshops cover a range of marine genomics and bioinformatics skills, including:

- sequence data handling and quality assessment
- command-line bioinformatics workflows
- genome and metagenome assembly
- genome binning
- genome completeness and contamination assessment
- taxonomic classification
- genome annotation
- metabolic pathway reconstruction
- interpretation of microbial community function

### Workshop 4 – Black Band Disease Metagenome

Workshop 4 focused on the analysis of a **Black Band Disease (BBD) metagenome** using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing data.

The workflow included:

- **Flye** – metagenome assembly
- **MetaBAT2** – genome binning
- **CheckM** – genome quality assessment
- **GTDB-Tk** – taxonomic classification
- **Prokka** – genome annotation
- **MinPath** – metabolic pathway reconstruction

The analysis recovered several microbial genome bins associated with the BBD community, including *Roseofilum* and several Desulfobacterota lineages. The results were also compared with previous Illumina-based BBD metagenomic research.
